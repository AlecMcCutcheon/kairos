Kairos wiki

Verifiable shared time for Freenet apps — not a service that syncs your laptop clock. Peers publish signed wall times; apps Get / Subscribe the contract and use either the live pulse map or sealed stamps, depending on the job. The Kairos site also runs bounded Tyche duty beside its own duty, using separate identities and trust domains so both public goods can warm together.

Two clocks, one contract

  • Pulse map — keep-alive. Median of current pulse observations. Moves when anyone pulses. Used by the OTP lab (hardened tip — early MFA surface). Never a notarize proof by itself.
  • Sealed stamps — open_stamp + aged / reputation-eligible observe_stamp → sealed_stamps[hash:nonce] with weighted median, error, [earliest, latest], transcript. Used for notarize and anti-replay that must stick.
Kairos does not replace Chrony/NTP on devices. It publishes Freenet state apps can Get. A Get is read-only — it does not write a pulse. Honest limits: Trust & security.

Soft time for neighboring services

Tyche may use Kairos’s aged, reputation-weighted pulse median as a labeled client-side time hint for round-age displays and coordination. It is deliberately a soft relationship: Tyche falls back to its local clock during bootstrap, staleness, skew, or outage, and signed Tyche contract observations remain authoritative for eligibility and consensus.

What Kairos is for

  • App clock — Get or Subscribe one network-agreed time with an explicit error / belief bound.
  • Notarize — seal a content hash into [earliest, latest] with a witness transcript.
  • Epoch bins / OTP — TOTP over pulse-map median (lab) or sealed time (production notarize paths).
